AI analysis

What the analysis found.

These findings describe the field; they do not rank it, and none of them is a recommendation.

Analysis performed by ChatGPT, Gemini and DeepSeek, fact-checked and consolidated by Claude · Sept 25, 2026

01

A crowded field with uneven information.

Four candidates hold or have held major office: Parrish, Crombie, Tedjo and Damerla. They receive most of the news coverage and were the four in the first televised debate. Detailed written platforms come from Damerla, Jilani, Matulewicz, Tedjo and Crombie. For Khan, Bhatti and Naami, almost nothing is on the public record. A thin profile reflects what's been published, not a judgment about the candidate.

02

Taxes are the sharpest divide.

Tedjo and Jilani propose freezing taxes, and Damerla proposes a freeze for 2027. Matulewicz targets a 5% operating-budget cut, Ahmed wants lower taxes, and Fang would “axe” property tax. Crombie rejects a freeze in favour of an audit and department cuts. Parrish has focused on regional and police cost growth. Keep in mind that a freeze on the city's share doesn't freeze your whole bill, which also includes Region of Peel and school board portions.

03

Four different ideas for transit.

Crombie proposes extending TTC Line 2 into downtown Mississauga. Damerla offers a cheaper alternative: more stops on the Lakeshore GO line as a "surface subway." Tedjo wants a rapid-transit grid and a Dundas East LRT. Matulewicz would fund more transit partly through a fuel levy. All of the major rail options depend on provincial or federal funding and partners such as Metrolinx.

04

Policing is also about who pays.

Peel police are funded through the Region of Peel. Parrish left the police board in a budget dispute, while Crombie and Jilani would seek a seat on it. Damerla wants the Mississauga–Brampton cost-sharing formula reviewed. Matulewicz proposes civilian responders for non-criminal calls. Tedjo is endorsed by the police association. Several candidates focus on auto theft.

05

Data centres are a live decision.

In September, council unanimously passed a one-year interim control bylaw on new data centres over 10 MW. Positions range from Tedjo's "no new data centres" and Vasquez De Espinal's support for a moratorium, to Damerla's opposition to subsidies and Matulewicz's call for safeguards. Crombie wants a clear position taken. Parrish did not vote on the July motion.

06

Several candidates want more oversight.

An independent Auditor General appears in Damerla's and Matulewicz's platforms, and Crombie proposes an independent spending audit. Jilani and Vasquez De Espinal propose plain-language tax reporting. Elliott wants developer meetings with council made public.

07

What a mayor can and can't do on their own.

Mississauga's mayor already holds Ontario's strong mayor powers, including proposing the budget. Even so, many commitments depend on council, the Region of Peel, the province or outside partners:

  • Tax freezes (Tedjo, Jilani, Damerla) apply to the city's share and still need spending decisions to match.
  • Eliminating property tax (Fang) would mean replacing the city's largest source of revenue, since Ontario municipalities must balance their budgets.
  • Subway and GO proposals (Crombie, Damerla) need provincial agreement and funding.
  • Police budgets and the funding formula (Damerla, Matulewicz, Jilani) are set through the Region of Peel and the police board, not by the city alone.
  • Parrish's housing record, such as the development-charge cuts, was achieved through council votes during her term.

Before you vote

Questions worth asking the candidates.

On money and powers

  1. If you promise a tax freeze or cut, what specific spending would change to pay for it?
  2. How would you use the mayor's strong mayor powers, and where would you choose not to use them?
  3. Which of your commitments need provincial, federal or Region of Peel approval or funding?

On the big local issues

  1. What should happen when the data-centre interim control bylaw expires?
  2. How would you change Peel police costs, or Mississauga's share of them?
  3. What, specifically, would you change in your first 100 days?

How this was put together

  • Candidate research was performed by three AI engines: ChatGPT, Gemini and DeepSeek.

  • The candidate list comes from the City of Mississauga's official certified list.
